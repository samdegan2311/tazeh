Questions about Tazeh, and how to reach us.

Alerts

Tazeh alerts you only to Top Stories: usually no more than two a day, hours apart, and none at night in Tehran unless there's an imminent danger. If none arrive, check that notifications are on for Tazeh in your iPhone's Settings and in Tazeh's own.

Football

Turn on Football in Tazeh's Settings and choose your teams to see their matches on Today, with Match Alerts and Live Activities. Turning football off stops all of it.

Prices

Markets shows the Tehran free market and 30 cryptocurrencies, priced in toman. Prices are for information only.

A story looks wrong

Every story lists the outlets that reported it. Open one to read the original; if our summary got it wrong, write to us.

Contact

Write to tazehapp@icloud.com. We read every message.
