Privacy Policy

Tazeh · تازه

Tazeh is built to know as little about you as possible. There's no account and no sign-in, and the app has no advertising or analytics in it.

What Tazeh sends

When you turn on notifications, Apple gives your iPhone a token for delivering alerts. Tazeh's server keeps that token, along with the alerts you've asked for — the teams you follow and full-time results — so the right ones reach you. The token doesn't say who you are. While a match follows you on the Lock Screen, its Live Activity has a token of its own, used only to keep it up to date.

What stays on your iPhone

Your watchlist, your appearance and haptics settings, and the stories saved for reading offline are kept on your iPhone and never leave it.

What Tazeh never does

Tazeh doesn't ask for your name, email, phone number, location or contacts. It doesn't track you across other apps or websites, and it doesn't sell or share anything about you.

Other services

Alerts are delivered by the Apple Push Notification service. Photos and source icons load from the publishers' own sites, and opening a story's source takes you to that publisher, whose own privacy policy applies there.

Your choices

You can turn notifications off at any time in your iPhone's Settings, and turn football off in Tazeh's. Deleting the app removes everything it kept on your iPhone, and its token stops working.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version appears here, with the date it takes effect.
